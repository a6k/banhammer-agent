Privacy Policy

BanHammer — Fail2Ban Monitor

Overview

BanHammer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no backend server, no analytics service, no crash reporter, and no third-party SDK that could receive your data.

Data Storage

BanHammer stores the following data exclusively on your device:

  • Your server URL(s) — saved in iCloud or locally, depending on your sync preference.
  • Your API key — stored in the device Keychain (encrypted, never transmitted anywhere except to your own server).

Optionally, if you enable iCloud sync, server configurations are synced via your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is only accessible to you and is governed by Apple's iCloud privacy practices.

Network Connections

BanHammer connects only to the server you configure. The connection is a direct HTTPS/WSS connection from your device to the banhammer-agent running on your own Linux server. No connection is made to any server operated by the developer of BanHammer.

Diagnostics & Analytics

BanHammer does not use any analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry service. If you opt in to Apple's app analytics through the App Store, aggregate and anonymized data may be shared with the developer through Apple's platform. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy and cannot be linked to you personally.

Children

BanHammer is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the privacy policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL. The date at the bottom of this page reflects the last update.
